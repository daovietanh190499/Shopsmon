What the assessment covers

Six parts, written for decision makers as well as engineers.

Use-case list

The time-consuming processes, an estimate of impact (volume, time, error rate) and a ranking by value and difficulty.

Data and documents

What data exists, where it lives, its quality and sensitivity, and what must still be collected to measure results.

Pilot design

A small scope, acceptance criteria measured on held-out real examples, and a human-review step where it is risky.

Risk and privacy

Where data is sent, who can access it, which model provider is used, and what happens when the model is wrong.

Running costs

An estimate of model costs (API or self-hosted) at your real volume, alongside the build effort.

Roadmap

Next steps at fixed scope: build the pilot, scale it, or stop if the measurements do not justify it.

Common use cases

The requests small and mid-sized businesses ask about most.

  • A chatbot that answers customer questions from the business own documents and policies — chatbot & agent page
  • Search and question answering over internal documents (procedures, contracts, handbooks)
  • Reading invoices, forms and emails into a spreadsheet — custom AI tools
  • Sorting tickets, customer feedback and reviews
  • Recurring reports compiled automatically from several sources
  • Sensor/IoT monitoring and anomaly detection on device data — technical example on the blog

Process

Four steps, each with a concrete output.

1. Short conversation

You describe the business, the process that hurts and what you hope for. I ask about anything unclear.

2. Collect examples

You send a few dozen real examples (sensitive data removed) and describe what a correct result looks like.

3. Written assessment

You receive the six-part assessment above with a pilot proposal at fixed scope and price.

Optional next step: build the pilot, measure it on your data, then hand over source code, documentation and a walkthrough. Payment by PayPal invoice.

Frequently asked questions

Can a small business with little data do this?

Many tasks (extraction, classification, question answering over documents) use existing models rather than training a custom one. What is needed is a few dozen real examples to measure results.

Will my data leave my premises?

It depends on the design: a model provider API or a model running on your own machine. I state where data goes before starting; see the Privacy Policy.

Does AI replace staff?

I design for assistance, with a human-review step where it is risky. Not a fit for automated decisions on health, legal, credit or employment matters without human oversight.

How much and how long?

It depends on scope, so I give a fixed price and timeline once I understand the request. Two or three sentences are enough to start.

Tell me about the process you want to improve

I will reply with clarifying questions and a proposed next step.
