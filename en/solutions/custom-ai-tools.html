Kinds of tools I build

The kinds of work that fit.

Document extraction

Read PDFs, invoice photos, forms or emails and put the required fields into a spreadsheet/CSV, with flags on the rows a human should check.

Classification & tagging

Sort tickets, customer feedback, reviews or catalogue items into your own labels, optionally with a confidence score.

Data cleaning & merging

Normalise names, dates and addresses; detect duplicate records; merge several sources. Every change is logged so it can be audited.

Reports & dashboards

Turn recurring numbers into a report or a simple web dashboard, with a natural-language summary where it helps.

Workflow automation

Connect steps you do by hand: read an email, call an API, update a sheet, send a notification — with a human approval step where it is risky.

Small CLI / web utilities

A small app that runs on your machine or a cheap server, with install instructions and full source.

How I work

Small, measurable, no lock-in.

Understand the current routine

You send 5–10 real examples (sensitive data removed) and describe the output you want.

Agree acceptance criteria

We agree how to measure: accuracy on held-out examples, processing time, number of rows that need review.

Demo, then deliver

You try the demo on your own data; once you agree you get the source, documentation and a walkthrough. Payment by PayPal invoice.

What I will tell you up front

  • AI is not 100% accurate: I measure accuracy on your examples and design the human-review step.
  • Not every task is worth automating — if a simple script is enough, I will say so.
  • Your data is used only for the project; if it must go to a third-party AI provider I will tell you first (see the Privacy Policy).

Not a fit for: automated decisions on health, legal, credit or employment matters without human oversight, or projects that need guaranteed outcomes.
