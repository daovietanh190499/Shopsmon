Processing pipeline

Six steps, all running as JavaScript and WebAssembly on your device.

  1. Face detectionMediaPipe Face Landmarker: 478 landmarks; eye centres and chin are used.
  2. Background removalMODNet (ONNX Runtime Web) predicts an alpha matte; edges refined with a guided filter.
  3. Straighten and cropRotates along the eye line and places crown and chin according to the head-size ratio.
  4. New backgroundForeground colour estimation (blur-fusion) removes old-background fringes before compositing on the chosen colour.
  5. SharpeningUnsharp mask with a suggested amount based on sharpness measured on the face region.
  6. Export300 DPI PNG or JPG, or a 10×15 cm / A4 sheet with multiple copies.

Privacy and requirements

  • Photos are processed entirely in the browser; no photo is uploaded to a server.
  • The first run downloads about 37 MB (models + runtime); afterwards the browser cache is used.
  • Requires a browser with WebAssembly (recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
  • Front-facing, well-lit, single person; the larger the face in the source photo, the sharper the ID photo.
