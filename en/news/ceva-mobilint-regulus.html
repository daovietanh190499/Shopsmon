Ceva and Mobilint: the REGULUS edge-AI SoC delivers 10 TOPS under 3 W for robots, drones and IoT

2026-11-03 · Source: IoT Now

According to IoT Now, Ceva announced that Mobilint has deployed Ceva's SensPro AI DSP and NeuPro-Nano NPU in its REGULUS SoC — the first production deployment of the NeuPro NPU family. The company says REGULUS delivers 10 TOPS at under 3 W with up to 8 GB of memory, aimed at robotics, drones, machine vision, smart cameras and IoT devices. The REGULUS-based MLD-R1 USB accelerator (previewed at COMPUTEX 2026) is now shipping, with support for PyTorch, ONNX, Hugging Face, TensorFlow, TFLite and Keras.
