Ceva and Mobilint: the REGULUS edge-AI SoC delivers 10 TOPS under 3 W for robots, drones and IoT

2026-11-03 · Summarised by Shopsmon · Source: IoT Now

According to IoT Now, Ceva announced that Mobilint has deployed Ceva's SensPro AI DSP and NeuPro-Nano NPU in its REGULUS SoC — the first production deployment of the NeuPro NPU family. The company says REGULUS delivers 10 TOPS at under 3 W with up to 8 GB of memory, aimed at robotics, drones, machine vision, smart cameras and IoT devices. The REGULUS-based MLD-R1 USB accelerator (previewed at COMPUTEX 2026) is now shipping, with support for PyTorch, ONNX, Hugging Face, TensorFlow, TFLite and Keras. Performance figures are the manufacturer's.

Why it matters

10 TOPS under 3 W puts vision AI within reach of smart cameras, drones and small robots. For an AIoT project the practical question is whether the chip's toolchain supports your model — PyTorch/ONNX support is a plus here.
