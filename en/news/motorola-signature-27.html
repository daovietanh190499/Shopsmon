Motorola Signature 27 launches globally Nov 5: Tandem OLED up to 12,000 nits, 200MP telephoto

2026-11-02 · Source: Android Authority

Per Android Authority, Motorola has officially confirmed some key specs: a 6.8-inch Tandem OLED display with a claimed 12,000 nits (the article puts the Galaxy S26 Ultra at about 2,600 nits), a 50MP Sony LYT-910 main camera with LOFIC, a 200MP telephoto and a 50MP ultrawide, Android 17 and a promise of seven years of software updates. Battery, charging speed and price will be revealed at launch.
