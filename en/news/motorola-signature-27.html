Motorola Signature 27 launches globally Nov 5: Tandem OLED up to 12,000 nits, 200MP telephoto

2026-11-02 · Summarised by Shopsmon · Source: Android Authority

Per Android Authority, Motorola has officially confirmed some key specs: a 6.8-inch Tandem OLED display with a claimed 12,000 nits (the article puts the Galaxy S26 Ultra at about 2,600 nits), a 50MP Sony LYT-910 main camera with LOFIC, a 200MP telephoto and a 50MP ultrawide, Android 17 and a promise of seven years of software updates. Battery, charging speed and price will only be revealed at launch; rumoured prices are not official. Brightness and zoom figures are the company's claims, not independently tested.

Why it matters

Announced specs (brightness, zoom) are only a starting point; real-world performance has to wait for independent reviews after the Nov 5 launch.
