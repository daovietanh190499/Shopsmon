Huawei launches the OSTA brand and the OSTA O1 Pro: a 16:9.5 OLED, Kirin 9030S, a 200 MP main camera, a 7,000 mAh battery and HarmonyOS 7 AI Pioneer Edition, from CNY 5,499

2026-11-20 · Source: GSMArena

According to GSMArena (Nov 20, 2026), Huawei announced its new OSTA sub-brand in China; Huawei executive Richard Yu said OSTA stands for Original Smart Technology Accessible. The first device is the OSTA O1 Pro, launched at an event on Nov 20 (14:30 China time) held together with the Stelato V8 vehicle launch; Huawei’s support page says pre-orders opened at 10:08 on Nov 17. According to Huawei’s product page, the O1 Pro has a 6.39-inch OLED display with a 16:9.5 aspect ratio, up to 120 Hz refresh rate, 6,500 nits local peak brightness (measured at 1% APL, Huawei lab data), a 96.1% screen-to-body ratio and Kunlun glass; Android Headlines gives the resolution as 2232 × 1320. According to Gizmochina, the bezels are 1.05 mm on all four sides, the phone is 6.68 mm thick, weighs about 200 g and comes in three colours (black, white, purple). The chip is the Kirin 9030S, with 12 GB/256 GB, 12 GB/512 GB, 16 GB/512 GB and 16 GB/1 TB memory configurations. The rear cameras are a 200 MP main camera (1/1.28-inch sensor, RYYB, part of second-generation Red Maple Imaging) and a 50 MP periscope telephoto with 3.7x optical zoom; according to Gizmochina and GSMArena the phone has no ultrawide camera, unlike the Huawei Pura X View; the front camera is 50 MP. According to Huawei, the silicon-carbon battery has a typical capacity of 7,000 mAh (rated 6,815 mAh), with wired charging up to 66 W and wireless charging up to 50 W; the phone is rated IP68 and IP69, supports eSIM and has a side-mounted fingerprint sensor (per GSMArena). It is the first phone to ship with HarmonyOS 7 AI Pioneer Edition: according to Huawei and Gizmochina, the operating system has a system-level AI screen (Xiaoyi AI screen) that takes users to the service they need, a Little Star assistant (小星芒) and Xiaoyi Space for long-term memory, plus a feature that generates a theme from a single image; according to TechNode (citing STAR Market Daily), the system connects more than 2,100 system functions and more than 500 ecosystem skills, supporting tasks across services such as Alipay, Xiaohongshu, JD.com and Taobao. On the hardware, GSMArena says the phone is essentially a rebadged Huawei Pura X View without the ultrawide lens and without satellite messaging, while adding a 16 GB RAM option (the Pura X View tops out at 12 GB). Introductory prices in China (according to Gizmochina, 200 yuan below the list price for each configuration): CNY 5,499 for 12 GB/256 GB (about $824 according to GSMArena), CNY 6,499 for 12 GB/512 GB, CNY 7,299 for 16 GB/512 GB and CNY 8,799 for 16 GB/1 TB; according to Gizmochina, sales started at 18:08 China time on Nov 20 on Vmall and JD. Huawei has not announced plans to sell OSTA devices outside China.
