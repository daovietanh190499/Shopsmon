Strategist: a meta-agent that evolves inference strategies as code, with 39% lower inference cost

2026-11-03 · Summarised by Shopsmon · Source: arXiv

Lu, Xu, Hua and Liu introduce Strategist, a meta-agent that writes inference strategies as “blueprints” — executable code that other strategies can reuse — and stores them in a library. For a given task and a small development set, it retrieves related blueprints, writes candidate strategies, tests them on real samples and revises them from the results. According to the authors' abstract, across 30 benchmarks Strategist beats the strongest baseline on 24; average accuracy is 74.7% vs 74.0% at 39% lower inference cost, and strategies transfer to unseen tasks and other models without further search. The results are the authors' own, without independent verification.

Why it matters

The core idea — separating the model from the inference strategy and treating the strategy as reusable code — matches how agents are built in practice: much of the quality and cost sits in the orchestration loop, not just the model.
