Xiaomi 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max launch globally: Privacy Display, a rear screen, two 200 MP Leica cameras and prices from €1,399

2026-11-17 · Source: Android Authority

According to Android Authority (Hadlee Simons, Nov 17, 2026), Xiaomi launched the Xiaomi 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max in global markets on Nov 17, after the two phones debuted in China in September; the article notes the company had not offered Pro phones outside China for several years. The Xiaomi 18 Pro uses the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 and the 18 Pro Max uses the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6; according to Notebookcheck both are built on TSMC’s 2 nm process with Oryon CPUs of two Prime and six Performance cores, with peak clock speeds of up to 5 GHz according to Xiaomi. According to Android Authority, the Extreme variant has a more powerful GPU, higher AI performance and supports video features such as 8K/60 fps, 4K/240 fps and the APV codec, but Xiaomi is not taking advantage of these. The main display is a 120 Hz LTPO OLED with 4,000 nits peak brightness, 6.4 inches (18 Pro) and 6.9 inches (18 Pro Max). According to Xiaomi, Privacy Display switches off standard pixels and keeps lit dedicated privacy pixels with a narrower viewing angle, limiting visibility from the sides; privacy mode can apply to selected apps, notifications, floating windows and split-screen views, and a Privacy Notice feature uses the front camera to vibrate the phone or warn on the display when someone else is looking at the screen. The back carries a secondary 120 Hz display with 4,000 nits peak brightness and Gorilla Glass Victus 2, used as a viewfinder for the rear camera, for AI translation during conversations, for notifications and for integration with selected services (YouTube Music, Spotify, Google Maps, Gemini Live); a GenAI Apps feature generates mini-apps from natural language with over 50 preset templates, and users can pin images or text such as QR codes and boarding passes to this screen. On cameras, according to Leica and Android Authority, both phones have a camera system co-engineered with Leica (Summilux optics): a 200 MP main camera with a 1/1.28-inch sensor at f/1.67, a 200 MP telephoto camera with a 1/1.56-inch sensor, 75 mm at f/2.4 and 12 cm close focus, a 50 MP 17 mm f/2.4 ultrawide camera with a 102° field of view, and a 50 MP autofocus selfie camera; the 18 Pro Max uses a Light Fusion 960L sensor with LOFIC HDR and a 16.2 EV dynamic range when shooting at 1x, while the 18 Pro lacks this feature. Video recording goes up to 8K at 30 fps or 4K at 120 fps in Dolby Vision with 10-bit log. The silicon-carbon battery (28% silicon) is 6,000 mAh on the 18 Pro (7,000 mAh in the Chinese version) and 7,050 mAh on the 18 Pro Max (8,500 mAh in the Chinese version); wired charging is 90 W (18 Pro) and 100 W (18 Pro Max), wireless charging is 50 W, with IP68 protection and an ultrasonic in-display fingerprint sensor. According to 9to5Google, the phones run HyperOS 4 (Android 17) and include 3 months of Google AI Pro. European pricing (according to Notebookcheck and 9to5Google): the Xiaomi 18 Pro starts at €1,399 (12 GB/256 GB) and €1,599 (12 GB/512 GB); the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max starts at €1,599 (12 GB/256 GB) and €1,799 (12 GB/512 GB); four colours: Black, White, Cosmic Blue and Velvet Red.
