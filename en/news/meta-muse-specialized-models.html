Meta moves Muse and Meta AI to specialised models trained with Muse Wonder: 2.4x less forgetting of critical information and 67% better completion of tasks that hit snags

2026-11-06 · Source: Meta AI Research

According to a Meta AI Research post (Nov 6, 2026), Meta began rolling out to Muse and Meta AI users specialised versions of its existing models, trained using Muse Wonder — the company's most capable model family, introduced on Oct 12 — rather than Muse Wonder itself. According to The New Stack, Meta has not named the new models, and Muse Wonder itself remains for paying subscribers' harder tasks. Two features that debuted with Muse Wonder are brought to these models. Active Memory helps a model remember what matters and recognise when earlier information becomes relevant; the models are trained to tell temporary information from permanent, to reason across time and to separate what is still relevant from what has gone stale. Active Focus helps Muse keep working toward a goal when it hits an obstacle by trying harder, recovering or finding alternatives; the models are trained on a variety of bottlenecks that reflect the real world. According to Meta, in Muse the improved models show a 2.4x reduction in forgetting critical information needed for the user's goals and a 67% improvement in completing everyday tasks where unexpected issues arise. On Meta's Active Memory evaluation, the current Muse model correctly used critical context in 90% of tasks (previous model: 76%); on Meta's Personal Superintelligence Index the score is 47% versus 38% for the previous model. These figures are published by Meta itself. Meta says the models also learned to both push on goals independently and collaborate live with the user, and to adapt when the user steers mid-task. In Meta AI, the same improvements let the assistant break complex problems into clear steps, ask sharper follow-up questions and keep up when the user changes direction; according to The New Stack, Meta AI runs inside WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Messenger.
