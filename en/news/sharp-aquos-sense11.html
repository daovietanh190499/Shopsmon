Sharp introduces the AQUOS sense11: about 157 g and 7.6 mm thick, a 5,000 mAh battery, a 6.2-inch Pro IGZO OLED at up to 3,000 nits, Snapdragon 7s Gen 3, on sale in Japan from Nov 26

2026-11-17 · Source: Sharp

According to Sharp (Nov 17, 2026), the AQUOS sense11 is the company’s new standard-model smartphone, weighing about 157 g and about 7.6 mm thick (151 × 73 × 7.6 mm) yet carrying a 5,000 mAh typical-capacity battery; by Sharp’s own criteria (10 hours of use a day made up of 4 hours of video, 3 hours of music, 2 hours of social media and 1 hour of gaming), the battery lasts about two days per charge, and its “Intelligent Charge” technology controls charging to reduce battery degradation. The phone uses the Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 (octa-core, 2.5 GHz + 2.4 GHz + 1.8 GHz), runs Android 17 and comes in two memory configurations: 8 GB RAM/128 GB ROM and 12 GB RAM/256 GB ROM (the latter only on the SIM-free model); the 8 GB of RAM is about 1.3 times that of the AQUOS sense10 according to Sharp’s research. The display is an approximately 6.2-inch Full HD+ (2,340 × 1,080) Pro IGZO OLED with a 1 to 240 Hz variable refresh rate (Sharp notes that the 240 Hz mode is achieved by inserting a black frame between frames in compatible apps and game settings) and a peak brightness of up to 3,000 nits, about 1.5 times the previous model. The rear cameras are an approximately 50.3 MP main camera (F1.9, 84° wide angle, optical image stabilisation) and an approximately 50.3 MP ultrawide camera (F2.2, 122°); the front camera is approximately 32.0 MP. Sharp says the camera has AI features that remove unwanted people from the background, remove shadows cast on food or text and reduce reflections when shooting through glass, a masking feature that makes text on signs unreadable to protect privacy (already on the AQUOS R11), all-pixel PDAF and HDR video recording. The phone comes with Sharp’s integrated AI service “NIAH” preinstalled, launched by pressing the power key twice, which lets users ask about the phone and home appliances by voice or text and manage schedules and household tasks. On durability, it is rated IPX5, IPX8 and IPX9 for water resistance and IP6X for dust, and complies with MIL-STD-810H (Sharp says 16 test items were run); the body and camera ring are aluminium; it supports six years of security updates from launch and up to four OS version upgrades. Other specifications: Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax), Bluetooth 5.2, face recognition (mask supported) and a fingerprint sensor, NFC/Osaifu-Keitai, nano SIM plus eSIM, a microSD slot, a newly developed slim W-Box speaker and the “Akarium” notification light. The phone goes on sale in sequence from Nov 26, 2026 through carriers and retailers in Japan (the date may vary by carrier), on the same day as a SIM-free model for the open market; Sharp Japan lists NTT Docomo, KDDI, SoftBank, Rakuten Mobile and J:COM, and the SIM-free model code is SH-M37. Sharp’s English release does not state a price.
