Google launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen: $1.50/$7.50 per million tokens, 57% on Terminal-Bench 4.1 and 2.5x faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash at the default thinking level

2026-11-12 · Source: Google

According to Google (Nov 12, 2026, a post by Koray Kavukcuoglu, SVP at Google DeepMind), Gemini 4 Hydrogen is a fast, efficient model for everyday coding and agentic workflows; the two frontier models, Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Argon, remain for deep, long-horizon reasoning on the hardest problems. Hydrogen accepts up to 1 million input tokens and generates up to 1 million output tokens, with flat pricing across the whole context window: $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens. The model has four thinking levels: low, medium, high (the default) and a new xhigh tier. According to Google, at the default high level Hydrogen scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, above Argon (50), and 54 at xhigh. Compared with Gemini 3.8 Flash, Google states: tasks finish 2.5 times faster at the default high setting, the Terminal-Bench 4.1 score more than triples (57% versus 18.2%) and fabricated claims fall by more than 5 times; in early testing across complex graphs, SQL and data-heavy enterprise workflows, partners saw Hydrogen run 2 to 2.8 times faster while using 12% to 23% fewer tokens. On evaluations, Google says Hydrogen ranks #5 on arena.ai’s Agent Arena, scores 58.8% on HLE-Diamond, ranks #1 on the Vals Tax Agent Bench (52.3%), ties for #1 on the Vals Legal Research Bench (55.8%), and scores 53.4% on the new Multimodal Knowledge Agent (MKA) benchmark and 71.6% on Agents’ Last Exam v1.1. The same day, Google introduced Orchestrator in Google Antigravity, which switches automatically between models such as Carbon and Hydrogen within one session: on Terminal-Bench 4.0, Orchestrator reached near-Carbon task success while using on average 24% less quota and finishing 19% faster. For workflows with parallel agents, Carbon can act as coordinator while Hydrogen powers worker and verifier agents in isolated workspaces. On safety, Google says Hydrogen cuts overreach in agentic tasks by up to 81% and under-service by up to 79%, reduces misaligned actions and task shortcutting 12-fold across tool-use and coding evaluations, is up to 2 times less likely to bypass permission boundaries when faced with impossible tasks, reaches a 94% honesty rate on an internal agent honesty evaluation and is more than 2.3 times more resilient to indirect prompt-injection attacks on Gray Swan IPI; false refusals on HealthBench Pro are under 0.4%. Hydrogen is available from Nov 12 on the Gemini API (Google AI Studio, Android Studio), Gemini for business, Gemini in Google Sheets (eligible plans), the Gemini app (model drop-down) and Google Search for all users; free users in the Gemini app get it through automatic model selection when it suits the prompt; Google Antigravity is for paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers. The figures are published by Google itself.
