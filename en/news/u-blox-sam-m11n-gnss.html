u-blox introduces the SAM-M11N: a 4.4 g GNSS module with a built-in antenna, as low as 10 mW for battery-powered trackers

2026-11-05 · Source: u-blox

According to u-blox's announcement (Nov 5, 2026), the SAM-M11N is a GNSS module built on the company's latest F11 platform that combines a receiver and an omnidirectional antenna in a single 15.5 × 15.5 × 5.3 mm module weighing 4.4 g. u-blox says it is 1 mm thinner and 0.9 g lighter than the SAM-M10Q and the thinnest and smallest by volume of any GNSS module with an integrated antenna. A high-gain 15.5 × 15.5 mm RHCP patch antenna is built in, so designers do not need a separate GNSS antenna and the associated RF components; pinout is compatible with the previous SAM line. The SAM-M11N is the first module in the antenna-integrated SAM family to support LEAP (Low Energy Accurate Positioning) mode: in the L1 band it consumes as little as 10 mW at 1.8 V. Horizontal accuracy is 1.5 m CEP, navigation update rates reach 10 Hz with three concurrent constellations or 25 Hz with one, and the supply range is 1.8–3.6 V. Applications named in the announcement include asset-tracking tags, livestock and pet trackers, personal trackers, lightweight drones and aftermarket telematics devices. The EVK-F112 evaluation kit can be used to explore the F11 platform and assess power consumption; the first SAM-M11N samples will be shown at electronica 2026 (Nov 10–13, Munich; Hall C4, booth 480).
