iPad mini with an 8.5-inch OLED display and the 2 nm A20 Pro goes on sale Nov 6: Apple cites up to 4.5x the AI performance of the previous generation and local LLMs

2026-11-06 · Source: Apple

According to Apple's press release (published Oct 26, 2026), the new iPad mini has an 8.5-inch OLED display in the same footprint thanks to 24% thinner bezels, is 5.4 mm thin and has an IP67 water and dust rating. It was available to pre-order from Oct 28 in 36 countries and regions, and began arriving to customers and reaching stores on Friday, Nov 6. The Super Retina XDR display reaches up to 1,200 nits peak brightness (Apple says more than 2x the previous generation), 600 nits full-screen for SDR and HDR, a 2,000,000:1 contrast ratio, a 1-nit minimum brightness and an optional nano-texture glass that reduces glare. The A20 Pro chip is built on a 2 nm process with a 7-core GPU that has a Neural Accelerator in each GPU core and up to a 6-core CPU. According to Apple, compared with the previous generation AI performance is up to 4.5x higher, graphics up to 2.7x and multithreaded CPU performance up to 60%; the company cites running on-device models in creative apps as well as local LLMs. The 512 GB configuration has a dual 16-core Neural Engine (double the processing power of the previous generation) and unified-memory bandwidth rises by more than 2x. The tablet uses the N1 networking chip (Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, Thread); cellular models use the C2 modem, up to 50% faster than the previous generation. A 12 MP Center Stage front camera sits on the landscape edge, a 12 MP rear camera records 4K at up to 60 fps, and an Acoustic Display places the speakers behind the screen. iPad mini runs iPadOS 27 with Apple Intelligence and Siri AI (beta). It comes in midnight and starlight with 128, 256 or 512 GB of storage; in the U.S. it starts at $699 (Wi-Fi) and $849 (Wi-Fi + Cellular).
