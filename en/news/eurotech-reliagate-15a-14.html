Eurotech expands its ReliaGATE 15A-14 Edge AIoT gateway family: a four-slot Extended version, 5G RedCap, LoRaWAN, a 2.3 TOPS NPU and IEC 62443-4-2 SL2 certification

2026-11-19 · Source: Eurotech

According to Eurotech’s announcement (Amaro, Italy, Nov 19, 2026), the company is expanding its compact, fanless and ventless ReliaGATE 15A-14 Edge AIoT gateway family with new features, connectivity options and certifications. The family is built on a modular architecture that enables application-specific configurations at a fraction of the cost and development time of a fully custom solution. ReliaGATE 15A-14 now comes in two form factors: Compact (the CPU module plus two expansion modules) and Extended (up to four slots, double the expansion capacity). Customers can choose pre-certified configurations or order build-to-order configurations from a library of connectivity and I/O modules that includes 5G RedCap, LoRaWAN, four-port serial and dual SFP fibre connectivity. According to the product page, modules also include a LoRa concentrator and a LoRa endpoint, isolated I/O, Ethernet RJ45 and LTE/GNSS/Wi-Fi; the 5G RedCap module falls back to LTE; the LoRa modules support LoRaWAN 1.0.3 and the concentrator is LoRa Basics Station v2 compatible. The common hardware platform uses an NXP i.MX 8M Plus Quad processor with an NPU for AI of up to 2.3 TOPS, 4 GB LPDDR4 and 32 GB eMMC, two Gigabit Ethernet ports, two USB 3.0, one RS-232/422/485, one CAN-FD, two digital inputs, two digital outputs and HDMI; power input is 12 or 24 VDC (range 9–30 V) with 3.5 W idle and 7.5 W typical consumption, and it operates from −25 to +70 °C fanless in an aluminium DIN-rail enclosure. On software, the device combines purpose-built hardware with Eurotech’s Everyware Software Framework (ESF): according to the announcement, ESF provides energy and grid protocols such as IEC 61850 (server and client), DNP3 and OCPP 1.6, and industrial protocols including Modbus, OPC UA, Siemens S7 and BACnet; according to the datasheet, ESF is Java/OSGi middleware with MQTT, web-based visual data-flow programming and Docker support, and ready-made connectors to Eurotech Everyware Cloud, AWS IoT Core and AWS IoT Greengrass. On security, according to Eurotech, the hardware and software architecture is certified to IEC 62443-4-2 Security Level 2, compliant with EN 18031-1 under the EU Radio Equipment Directive (RED) and designed for the EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA); the datasheet adds TPM 2.0, TrustZone and an always-on physical anti-tamper monitor. US and European cellular carrier certifications let the same device be deployed across regions. Livio Paradiso, Chief Product & Business Development Officer at Eurotech, said customers should not have to choose between an off-the-shelf gateway that almost fits and the cost and time of developing a custom solution; in his words, they want application-specific configurations built on an established platform. According to the announcement, both the Compact and Extended versions are available now.
