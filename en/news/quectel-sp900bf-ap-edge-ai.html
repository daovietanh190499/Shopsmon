Quectel introduces the SP900BF-AP at electronica 2026: an edge AI module on the Qualcomm Dragonwing IQ-9075 with up to 100 TOPS and a lifecycle of up to 10 years

2026-11-11 · Source: Quectel / Business Wire

According to Quectel Wireless Solutions' announcement (Business Wire, Munich, Nov 11, 2026), the SP900BF-AP is a smart module that lets robots, inspection systems and edge gateways run large AI models on the device itself. It is built on the Qualcomm Dragonwing IQ-9075 processor and delivers up to 100 TOPS of on-device AI compute, which lets industrial OEMs integrate AI directly into products while reducing the latency, bandwidth cost and data-privacy risks associated with cloud processing. The configuration includes an octa-core Qualcomm Kryo CPU running at up to 2.36 GHz, an Adreno 663 GPU, a Hexagon DSP and a dedicated NPU; memory options reach 36 GB LPDDR5X and 256 GB UFS, supporting multi-stream video processing and multiple displays. The applications named are autonomous mobile robots (with four-camera support, 3D perception and on-device navigation), production-line inspection (real-time defect detection without moving sensitive data off-site) and edge AI gateways running language and vision-language models such as Qwen, DeepSeek, Gemma and LLaVA locally. PCIe, USB 3.1, UART, SPI and I²C interfaces let OEMs add 4G/5G or Wi-Fi for each deployment. Quectel commits to a product lifecycle of up to 10 years; the module is designed to run Ubuntu and Yocto Linux, with support in development. According to Quectel, the module has completed key technical validation and engineering samples are available to selected customers for solution evaluation and joint development. Quectel's Chinese-language release on PR Newswire Asia the same day adds a figure of up to 200 TOPS of sparse compute and expansion from four to as many as 16 cameras. Quectel is exhibiting at stand C2.601 at electronica (Messe München, Nov 10–13, 2026).
