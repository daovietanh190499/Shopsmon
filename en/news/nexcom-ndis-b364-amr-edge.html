NEXCOM introduces the NDiS B364 Duro Edge: a fanless edge computer for autonomous mobile robots, Intel Core Series 3 (Wildcat Lake), up to 41 TOPS, 12 V to 24 V DC input

2026-11-20 · Source: NEXCOM

According to NEXCOM (Nov 20, 2026), the NDiS B364 Duro Edge is a fanless edge AI computer for autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) in factories and warehouses. It uses Intel Core Series 3 processors based on the Wildcat Lake architecture with an integrated NPU, in three options: Core 7 processor 350, Core 5 processor 320 and Core 3 processor 304. According to NEXCOM, total AI performance reaches up to 41 TOPS across the CPU, GPU and NPU (an 18-TOPS Xe3 GPU, an 18-TOPS NPU and a 5-TOPS CPU), nearly double the previous Arrow Lake platform (22.5 TOPS), with the Xe3 GPU alone more than 1.5 times faster; the company targets tasks such as object recognition, image analysis and obstacle detection. The computer runs from a 12 V to 24 V DC input and operates from -20 °C to 60 °C. The configuration includes one DDR5-6400 SO-DIMM slot for up to 64 GB, two HDMI 1.4 ports (up to 4096×2160 at 30 Hz), three Intel LAN ports (two 2.5GbE and one 1GbE), one USB 3.2 Gen 2, one USB 3.2 Gen 1 and two USB 2.0 ports, plus TPM 2.0 and Intel ISM technology. The AMR-oriented interfaces include USB 3.2 for 3D cameras, RS-422/485 for motor drivers and the battery management system (BMS), GPIO for status inputs and alert outputs, high-bandwidth LAN for 3D LiDAR sensors, and an audio output that is line-out by default and can be customised to speaker-out for voice announcements. It has three M.2 sockets: Key B 3052/3042 for an optional 5G/LTE module, Key E 2230 for an optional Wi-Fi module (which can also host a third-party CAN FD module) and Key M 2242 for an optional SSD; NEXCOM says the wireless links serve communication with fleet dispatch systems, warehouse management systems (WMS), remote monitoring platforms and OTA updates. NEXCOM cites a forecast that the AMR market will reach USD 21.4 billion by 2033. The ordering information lists NDiS B364-350 (10W90E30100X0) and NDiS B364-320 (10W90E30101X0); the release does not state a price or availability date.
