Ezurio launches the GW16168: a USA-made M.2 AI accelerator with the NXP Ara240, up to 40 eTOPS, 8 GB or 16 GB of LPDDR4, -40 °C to +85 °C

2026-11-19 · Source: Ezurio

According to Ezurio (Akron, Ohio, Nov 19, 2026), the GW16168 is a USA-designed and USA-made M.2 AI accelerator built around the NXP Ara240 discrete neural processing unit (DNPU). The card delivers up to 40 equivalent tera operations per second (eTOPS) of on-device AI in a standard M.2 M-Key 2280 form factor (22.0 × 80 × 3.5 mm) and is available from the day of the announcement. It is purpose-built for the VeniceFLEX and Catalina single board computers and also works with other compatible M.2 M-Key 2280 platforms. The key specifications according to Ezurio are 8 GB or 16 GB of LPDDR4, a PCIe Gen4 x4 interface with a single 3.3 V supply, an industrial temperature range of -40 °C to +85 °C, Secure Boot and Root of Trust, and TensorFlow, PyTorch and ONNX model support through the NXP Ara SDK. Ezurio says the card runs AI models entirely on the device with no connection to the cloud, which suits drones, robots and remote industrial equipment that operate beyond reliable network coverage, and applications where latency, privacy or security rule out sending data off-site; it enables real-time machine vision, large language model (LLM) and vision language model (VLM) inference, and intelligent automation. The company stresses that the USA design and manufacturing gives industrial, defense and other supply-chain-sensitive customers added transparency, quality control and long-term availability. The GW11062 evaluation kit pairs a Catalina board with a GW16168 accelerator (GW11062-1 with 16 GB, GW11062-2 with 8 GB) and comes preloaded with an Ubuntu image to shorten setup. Eric Singer, Ezurio product manager, says edge AI is moving from pilot to production. The GW16168 was originally part of the Gateworks portfolio and is now fully supported by Ezurio, which acquired Gateworks in June 2026. The GW16168 embedded module and the GW11062 kits are available now; the release does not state a price.
