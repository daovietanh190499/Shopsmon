OpenAI ships GPT-6.1 Terra: half the price of GPT-6.1 Sol, 1.05M-token context

2026-10-26 · Summarised by Shopsmon · Source: LLM Stats

Per the LLM Stats newsletter, GPT-6.1 Terra is a fast model for computer use, vision and writing, priced at $1 / $5 per million input / output tokens (50% below GPT-6.1 Sol) with a 1.05M-token context window. OpenAI says it matches GPT-6 Astra on Agents' Last Exam at medium effort for one-tenth the price per token. That is OpenAI's claim.

Why it matters

Half the per-token price plus a context window beyond a million tokens changes the cost equation for chatbots and long-document agents. Still, test the hallucination rate on real data before switching.
